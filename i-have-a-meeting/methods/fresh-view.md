# Fresh view

**Use when:** two hypotheses died in a row, or 5 minutes passed without new evidence.

**Move:** Write problem for another worker as symptoms only: loop command, expected vs actual, what was ruled out. Writing often shows gap. Else hand off, take independent work.

**Done when:** write-up exists; it gave next probe or someone else holds it.

**Trap:** sending your theory instead of symptoms. Helper inherits your blind spot.

**Source:** David Agans, *Debugging*, 2002, rule 8 "Get a Fresh View": report symptoms, not theories (via reviewers). Kernighan & Pike, *The Practice of Programming*, 1999, ch. 5: explain your code to someone else. Trigger thresholds are ours.
