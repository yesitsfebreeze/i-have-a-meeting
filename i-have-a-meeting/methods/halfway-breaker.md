# Halfway breaker

**Use when:** half of claimed estimate has passed.

**Move:** Cause confirmed (downhill) or still unknown (uphill)? Uphill: no extension. Cut scope (could vision checks pass without this? what happens if not done?), mitigate, or release claim with loop and ranked hypotheses; pick next. Inside an issue, do scariest unknown first.

**Done when:** at halfway: fix under way on confirmed cause, scope cut and recorded, or claim released with handoff.

**Trap:** "almost there". One stuck issue eats whole deadline.

**Source:** Ryan Singer, *Shape Up*, 2019, ch. 8 (https://basecamp.com/shapeup/2.2-chapter-08): "If they don't finish, by default the project doesn't get an extension." Ch. 13 and 14: uphill vs downhill, scariest work first, scope hammering. Halfway trigger is ours, not Singer's.
